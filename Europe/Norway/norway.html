Norway

Norway is a northern nation carved by ice and sea — defined by fjords, maritime tradition, and a modern society built on resource management and social trust.

Region: Northern Europe (Nordic)

Time Zone: UTC +1 (UTC +2 DST)

Population: ~5.5 million

Capital: Oslo

Area: 385,207 km²

Language: Norwegian

Currency: Norwegian Krone (NOK)

Culture: Nordic values & maritime heritage

Fjords & Mountains

Norway’s dramatic coastline is shaped by deep fjords carved by glaciers during the Ice Age.

Quick fact: Norway has one of the longest coastlines in the world.

Viking Age

Norse seafarers explored, traded, and raided across Europe and the North Atlantic.

Union Periods

Norway spent centuries in political unions with Denmark and later Sweden.

Independence

Norway gained full independence in 1905 through peaceful separation.

Quick fact: Norway became independent in 1905.

World War II

German occupation during World War II strengthened Norway’s postwar democratic resolve.

Oil Discovery

North Sea oil transformed Norway’s economy in the late 20th century.

Sovereign Wealth

Norway manages oil revenues through the world’s largest sovereign wealth fund.

Quick fact: The fund is worth over a trillion dollars.

Social Model

High trust, strong welfare systems, and equality define Norwegian society.

Environment & Energy

Hydropower dominates domestic energy, while climate responsibility remains a national debate.

Global Role

Norway plays an active role in diplomacy, peace mediation, and international development.

The Road Ahead

Norway’s future depends on transitioning beyond oil while preserving prosperity and social cohesion.
