Sweden

Sweden is a Nordic nation shaped by forests, lakes, and social innovation — known for balancing economic competitiveness with strong welfare, equality, and democratic stability.

Region: Northern Europe (Nordic)

Time Zone: UTC +1 (UTC +2 DST)

Population: ~10.6 million

Capital: Stockholm

Area: 450,295 km²

Language: Swedish

Currency: Swedish Krona (SEK)

Culture: Social equality & Nordic pragmatism

Forests & Lakes

Sweden’s landscape is dominated by forests, lakes, and long coastlines along the Baltic Sea.

Quick fact: Over half of Sweden is covered by forest.

Viking Heritage

Viking traders and explorers connected Sweden to Europe, Asia, and the Middle East.

Medieval Kingdom

Sweden emerged as a unified kingdom during the Middle Ages.

Great Power Era

In the 17th century, Sweden became a major European military power.

Quick fact: Sweden once controlled territory around the Baltic Sea.

Neutrality

Sweden avoided direct involvement in major wars for over two centuries.

Industrialization

Rapid industrial growth transformed Sweden into a modern economy.

Welfare State

Sweden developed one of the world’s most comprehensive welfare systems.

Innovation & Design

Technology, sustainability, and minimalist design define Swedish global influence.

Modern Society

High trust, equality, and education shape everyday life.

European Role

Sweden plays an active role in EU policy, climate leadership, and innovation.

The Road Ahead

Sweden’s future lies in sustainability, integration, and technological leadership.
