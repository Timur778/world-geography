Vatican City

Vatican City is the world’s smallest sovereign state — a spiritual and political center of global Catholicism, defined by faith, continuity, and symbolic power far exceeding its size.

Region: Southern Europe (Rome)

Time Zone: UTC +1 (UTC +2 DST)

Population: ~800

Capital: Vatican City

Area: 0.49 km²

Language: Latin, Italian

Currency: Euro (EUR)

Culture: Catholic tradition & sacred governance

Smallest State

Vatican City is the smallest independent country in the world by both area and population.

Quick fact: You can walk across the country in minutes.

Spiritual Center

It serves as the spiritual headquarters of the Roman Catholic Church.

The Papacy

The Pope serves as both religious leader and head of state.

Quick fact: The Pope is elected by the College of Cardinals.

Lateran Treaty

Vatican City became an independent state in 1929 through the Lateran Treaty with Italy.

Governance

Vatican City is an absolute elective monarchy.

Swiss Guard

The Swiss Guard has protected the Pope since the 16th century.

Quick fact: Guards must be Swiss Catholic males.

Art & Architecture

Vatican museums and St. Peter’s Basilica house some of the world’s greatest art.

Global Influence

Despite its size, Vatican City holds major diplomatic and moral influence.

Economy

Revenue comes from tourism, donations, and philatelic and numismatic sales.

Modern Role

The Vatican engages in global dialogue on ethics, peace, and humanitarian issues.

The Road Ahead

Vatican City’s future lies in maintaining spiritual leadership in a changing world.
