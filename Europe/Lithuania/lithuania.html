Lithuania

Lithuania is a Baltic nation forged by medieval power, cultural endurance, and modern reintegration into Europe after decades of foreign domination.

Region: Northern Europe (Baltic)

Time Zone: UTC +2 (UTC +3 DST)

Population: ~2.8 million

Capital: Vilnius

Area: 65,300 km²

Language: Lithuanian

Currency: Euro (EUR)

Culture: Baltic roots & historical resilience

Forests & Plains

Lithuania’s landscape is dominated by forests, lakes, and flat plains, shaping rural traditions.

Quick fact: Lithuania has over 6,000 lakes.

Last Pagans

Lithuania was the last pagan state in Europe before converting to Christianity.

Grand Duchy

The Grand Duchy of Lithuania once stretched from the Baltic to the Black Sea.

Quick fact: Lithuania was Europe’s largest state in the 15th century.

Polish Union

Political union with Poland shaped language, religion, and governance.

Imperial Rule

Lithuania was absorbed into the Russian Empire in the 18th century.

First Republic

Lithuania declared independence in 1918 after World War I.

Soviet Occupation

Decades of Soviet rule suppressed culture and independence.

Quick fact: Lithuania was annexed by the USSR in 1940.

Restored Independence

Lithuania became the first Soviet republic to declare independence in 1990.

European Integration

Membership in the EU and NATO anchors Lithuania’s security and economy.

Culture & Identity

Folk songs, language preservation, and historical memory define national identity.

The Road Ahead

Lithuania’s future depends on innovation, demographic stability, and regional security.
