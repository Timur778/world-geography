Costa Rica

Costa Rica is a country that chose stability over force — a nation that invested in peace, education, and nature instead of armies and power projection.

Region: Central America (North America)

Time Zone: UTC −6

Population: ~5.2 million

Capital: San José

Area: 51,100 km²

Language: Spanish

Currency: Costa Rican Colón

Culture: Peace, nature & civic trust

Geography of Biodiversity

Costa Rica sits between two oceans and multiple mountain ranges, creating one of the most biologically diverse regions on Earth.

Quick fact: Costa Rica contains nearly 6% of the world’s biodiversity.

Colonial Simplicity

Unlike plantation-heavy colonies, Costa Rica developed with small farms and fewer entrenched elites, shaping a more equal society.

Abolition of the Military

In 1948, Costa Rica abolished its army, redirecting resources toward education, healthcare, and democratic institutions.

Quick fact: Costa Rica has had no standing army for over 70 years.

Democratic Stability

Continuous democratic governance created strong public trust and institutional continuity.

Environmental Leadership

Conservation policies transformed forests from exploitation zones into protected national parks.

Modern Economy

Tourism, technology, and sustainable agriculture drive economic growth with global integration.

Social Development

Education and healthcare access remain core pillars of social cohesion and opportunity.

Urban & Rural Balance

San José anchors governance while rural regions retain strong community structures.

Global Image

Costa Rica is internationally associated with peace, environmentalism, and quality of life.

The Road Ahead

The future depends on sustaining ecological leadership while managing urban growth and inequality.
